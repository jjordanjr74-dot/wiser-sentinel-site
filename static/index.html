Safe Chemical Storage & GHS Labeling Rules

Simple, foolproof rules for industrial warehouses, automotive shops, and commercial facilities:

Rule 1: Acid & Base Barrier

NEVER store Acids (Hydrochloric, Sulfuric) and Bases (Ammonia, Lye) together. Violent boiling neutralization splatters acid.

Rule 2: Oxidizer Distance

Keep Oxidizers (Bleach, Nitric Acid) at least 20 feet away from Flammables (Gasoline, Solvents). Contact causes spontaneous flash fires.

Rule 3: Water-Reactive Isolation

Water-reactives must be in dedicated dry storage with NO overhead water sprinkler heads (use dry chemical suppression).
