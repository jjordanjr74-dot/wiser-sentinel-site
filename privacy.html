Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WISER 2.6 Ultimate HazMat & SDS Sentinel ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit https://jjordanjr74-dot.github.io/wiser-sentinel-site/, install our Progressive Web Application, or use our official Android application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection and transparent practices:

  • Device & Usage Data: Standard server logs, operating system, browser type, and page access timestamps to ensure uptime and platform security.
  • Location Data (Optional): If you grant location permission in the app, location is processed entirely on your local device to calculate distances to nearby vendors, food trucks, or emergency zones. Location is never saved, tracked, or sold to third parties.
  • Camera & Files (Optional): Camera access is requested only when you actively choose to upload or scan a photo (such as container labels in WISER). Photos are processed locally or in memory and are never used for advertising.
  • Communications: When you call, text, or email through provided links, information you share is used solely to respond to your inquiry or fulfill your booking/order.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Deliver fast, reliable directory and app services.
  • Process direct booking inquiries, rentals, and customer service requests.
  • Maintain system security, prevent fraud, and verify service integrity.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We do not use third-party cross-site behavioral tracking networks. Standard infrastructure providers (e.g. Cloudflare for DDoS protection and hosting) process network traffic in compliance with industry privacy standards.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Services are intended for general audiences and commercial party rentals, dining guides, and professional reference.

5. Security & Retention

All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS). We retain minimal operational data only for as long as necessary to deliver verified services.

6. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to or deletion of any personal data you have submitted. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us through the official contact channels on https://jjordanjr74-dot.github.io/wiser-sentinel-site/.
